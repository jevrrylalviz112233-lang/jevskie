Goals

Name: MY GOAL TO ACHIVE A GOOD LIFE AND CHERISH MY TIME WITH MY LOLA

EXPLANATION

What I mean by achieving a good life and cherishing my time with my Lola is that I want to give her the life she never had the chance to experience. I realized that I don't need to be angry at my parents or guardians for not giving me the life I wanted, because they, too, never had the opportunity to experience the things they may have wished for. I came to this realization and understood that success isn't necessarily about achieving the dream job I once envisioned. Instead, it is about the outcome of everything I worked hard for, even when I expected nothing in return. Just like our parents and guardians, who may not have experienced the life they wanted, they still had strong faith, remained steadfast, and continued moving forward despite life's challenges. My goal is to build a better life, find a good job, and eventually raise my own family, while giving my Lola the happiness and comfort she deserves. I want to cherish the time I have with her and make the most of the opportunities I have, not just for myself, but also for the people I love.
